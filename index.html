Our Ruined Engagement Day by Blushing Bride

An Engagement Night Ruined: January 24th

We booked the photography services of Blushing Bride (blushingbride.co.in) for one of the most important days of our lives—our engagement on Jan 24th. We trusted them and paid them a massive ₹55,000 for professional service and unforgettable memories.

Instead, what followed was a complete disaster.

  • Broken Promises: When we first complained about the poor quality, we were promised it would be 'fixed' in the editing process. We've finally seen the edited photos, and they are just as bad—blurry, grainy, and completely amateurish.
  • Zero Direction: The photographer had no idea how to guide us. He was just clicking random pictures with zero sense of posing, lighting, or framing.
  • Missed Moments: They completely failed to capture the 'getting ready' shots and other essential, irreplaceable parts of the event.
  • No Delivery: It’s been nearly two months, and we still haven’t received our edited videos. Worse, when we posted an honest review of their terrible service, they completely denied giving us any of our remaining deliverables in retaliation.

Save your money and your memories—stay far away from this service. They ruined the photos of our special day, and below is exactly the "professional" quality our ₹55,000 bought us.

How The Scam Works

Don't fall for the facade they present online.

💸

Taking the Money

They take high advance payments under the pretext of 'booking confirmation', only to severely underdeliver or fail to show up as promised.

📵

Zero Accountability

Once things go wrong, their customer service vanishes. Calls go unanswered, messages are ignored, and refunds are impossible to get.

📸

Amateur Execution

Despite their polished website, the actual photographers sent are often unskilled, resulting in terrible, unusable photos of your big day.
